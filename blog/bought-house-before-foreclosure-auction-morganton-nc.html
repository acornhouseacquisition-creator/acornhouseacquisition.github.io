The Situation

By the time we met the owners, the foreclosure process was well underway and the sale date was set. They had two problems at once: not enough time, and a house in no shape to put on the market.

Listing it with an agent would have meant repairs first, then weeks of showings, then hoping a buyer's loan got approved before the auction. There wasn't time for any of that.

The House, As We Found It

From the road, the yard had grown over and vines were climbing the siding. Inside and under the house, the bigger issues showed up:

  • A long horizontal crack running across the basement wall.
  • Moisture staining on the block foundation walls.
  • Old framing and exposed wiring in the basement.
  • Flooring torn out down to the subfloor and damaged drywall in a bedroom.
  • A dated bathroom and an overgrown yard on all sides.

Why a Regular Buyer Couldn't Buy It

Most buyers use a mortgage, and lenders have rules about condition. A foundation crack and moisture problems usually get flagged at appraisal or inspection, and the loan stalls or dies. Even buyers who love the house often can't close on it.

That's why houses like this sit, and why owners in foreclosure run out of time. A cash buyer doesn't need a lender's approval, so the condition doesn't stop the sale.

How the Deal Worked

  1. We looked at the house and made a cash offer based on its real condition.
  2. The offer covered their mortgage payoff, plus money left over for the owners.
  3. We opened title right away and got the lender's payoff, since the auction date set the deadline.
  4. We closed before the auction. The lender was paid off at closing, the foreclosure sale didn't happen, and the owners received the rest in cash.
"With foreclosure, the clock matters more than the condition. We can handle any condition. What we need is enough time to get the payoff and the title work done." — Daniel Ghinda, Acquisition Manager, Acorn House Acquisition

"Leave Whatever You Don't Want"

This was the part the owners were happiest about. Years of belongings were still in the house, and sorting and hauling all of it in a few days, on top of moving, would have been overwhelming.

So we told them: take what matters to you and leave the rest. Furniture, boxes, things in the basement and the shed. We took care of the cleanout after closing, at no cost to them. They could focus on moving forward instead of renting dumpsters.

We Buy Houses in This Condition

This house isn't unusual for us. It's exactly the kind of house we're looking for. If your house needs work, we're the solution: we buy it fast and pay cash. That includes houses with:

  • Foundation cracks, water or moisture problems
  • Old wiring, plumbing, roofs or HVAC
  • Missing flooring, damaged walls or unfinished projects
  • Overgrown yards, sheds and outbuildings full of stuff
  • Foreclosure, back taxes, liens or an auction date
  • Furniture and belongings you don't want to move

You don't repair, clean, stage or haul anything. We give you a written cash offer within 24 hours, pay the normal closing costs, and close on your schedule, as soon as 7 days when there's a deadline.
