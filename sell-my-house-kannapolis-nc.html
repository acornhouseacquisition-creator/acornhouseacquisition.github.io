Selling a House in Kannapolis, NC: What to Know

Where things are handled for Kannapolis homeowners, and what that means for a fast sale.

  • Two counties. Kannapolis is split between Cabarrus County (seat: Concord) and Rowan County (seat: Salisbury). Records depend on which side your house is on.
  • Mill houses. Original wiring, plumbing and piers are common. We buy them as they are.
  • Foreclosure & probate. NC foreclosures go through a hearing before the Cabarrus County Clerk of Superior Court in Concord, followed by a sale and a 10-day upset bid period; paying off the loan before that ends can still stop it. Estates are opened with the Clerk's Estates Division in Concord.
